# Pitch: Backchannel (3 minutes)

## 1. The problem (30 seconds)

Look around this room. About 200 people, one main stage, mentors in side rooms, teams forming as we speak. How many of us missed an announcement today because we were heads-down coding? How many found the room too loud, or the speaker too fast, or the talk not in our first language? And how many people here would have been the perfect teammate, if we had only known?

## 2. The product (90 seconds, live)

1. **Organizer view.** I start captions from my microphone and go live on camera. My words appear on the video as captions.
2. **Participant view** on a second screen. The captions arrive instantly, and the whole transcript is searchable.
3. **Chat with the AI.** "@ai when is lunch?" It answers from what was actually said, and shows when it was said. Ask something that was never said, and it says so. No guessing.
4. **Español.** One click and the captions translate, on the device.
5. **Rooms.** Focus groups for topics, so mentors and attendees find each other. Team zones are private rooms with a join code, for people with aligned interests to build together.
6. **Built for everyone.** Pause without losing your place, captions-only layout, larger text, stronger contrast, keyboard and screen reader support. Not a mode you switch on. It is how the product works.

## 3. Built on the sponsor tools (45 seconds)

- **Elasticsearch** stores and searches every word. Next: hybrid vector search across every room and day.
- **Dash0** traces every caption from microphone to screen and measures caption lag. Next: a lag SLO per room, and Agent0 warning organizers before attendees notice.
- **Gitar** reviewed every pull request, and we fixed what it found in the same PRs.
- **Radar** is where it runs next: a Radar-connected cluster, with AI investigations when a captions pod fails mid-talk.

## 4. Close (15 seconds)

At a conference, the most valuable things are said once and then lost. Backchannel keeps them, translates them, answers questions about them, and connects the people who heard them.
